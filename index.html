Hyeonwoo Kim (김현우)

AI Research Engineer at Naver Corp

I work on large language models — post-training (SFT & alignment tuning), mathematical reasoning, and multilingual chat. Previously at Upstage, I contributed to the Solar model series (SOLAR 10.7B, Solar Pro), built MathGPT, and developed and operated the Open Ko-LLM Leaderboard for evaluating Korean LLMs. Before moving to LLMs, I spent several years building production recommender systems. I am also a Kaggle Master with a long record of machine learning competitions.
